## OBJECTIVE

Develop and demonstrate a small blockchain-based solution for the assigned real-world problem. The activity assesses application of blockchain concepts, executable smart-contract/prototype work, self-research, and discussion of security, limitations and future research.

## YOUR ASSIGNED PROBLEM

## College Reward Point System

Design a blockchain-based reward-point system in which a college can issue reward points to students and transfer them between accounts.

## EXPECTED SOLUTION FOCUS

- Solidity smart contract for token balance and transfer.

- ERC20 is the main token standard to investigate.

- Demonstrate account balance and token transfer.

- Use Ganache/Remix and MetaMask where appropriate.

- Research DApp/Web3.js connection.

## PHASE 1 – PROBLEM ANALYSIS AND BLOCKCHAIN DESIGN (4 Marks)

## Prepare:

- Problem statement and objective.

- Why blockchain is relevant.

- Clear architecture/workflow diagram.

- Role of relevant Ethereum components: Account, Transaction, Ether, Gas, EVM, Node and Blockchain.

Deliverable: Problem statement + architecture/workflow diagram + component mapping.

## PHASE 2 – PROTOTYPE / CODE EXECUTION AND DEMONSTRATION (6 Marks)

- Implement at least one application-specific Solidity smart contract or relevant executable blockchain component.

- Execute the relevant function/transaction using Remix, Ganache, MetaMask or another justified environment.


- Demonstrate the resulting state/balance/ownership/record/verification result.

- Show and explain the source code used in the live demonstration.

- Screenshots may support the report, but live execution should be demonstrated wherever applicable.

Suggested demonstration sequence: Create/allocate reward points → execute token transfer → show transaction → check updated balance.

## PHASE 3 – TECHNOLOGY INTEGRATION AND SELF-RESEARCH (4 Marks)

- Study ERC20 token functions relevant to the prototype.

- Study Ganache/Geth/Truffle.

- Study DApp architecture and Web3.js.

- Discuss Hyperledger Fabric as an alternative private/enterprise approach.

- Propose one Cloud/AI/IoT/ERP integration.

Prepare: Technology | Used/Proposed | Purpose | Reason for Selection.

## PHASE 4 – SECURITY, LIMITATIONS AND RESEARCH CHALLENGE (2 Marks)

Discuss any TWO relevant issues:

- Unauthorized token transfer

- Private-key/wallet security

- Smart-contract vulnerabilities

- Gas/transaction cost

- Scalability

Also state ONE research challenge or future research question related to your system.

## FINAL STUDENT DELIVERABLE

- Part A - Problem, objective, blockchain justification and architecture.

- Part B – Source code/prototype with evidence of execution.

- Part C  Technology research and selection table.

- Part D - Two security/limitation points and one research challenge.

- Submit ONE PDF report along with source code/prototype files.

## PRESENTATION AND VIVA

Duration: 5–7 minutes + Viva

- Problem and reason for blockchain.

- What changed after execution and how the transaction was processed.

- Technology choices.

- Research challenge.
